The Seven Best Used Compact SUVs on Sale in 2026

By Jason Allan · Founder: Bestest · Former: Head of vehicle reviews and ratings at Kelley Blue Book · Updated October 2026

Only 7 of the 13 compact SUVs Bestest scores qualify as good enough for Bestest shoppers. We've driven and evaluated each and every one of these SUVs, and we've been tracking their reliability, safety and resale value for a couple decades in some cases. With the most important research and vetting already done, you're free to choose the one that's as roomy or comfy or sporty or rugged as you want.

The Gold Standards

Toyota RAV4

Toyota RAV4

The highly regarded and wildly popular RAV4 pairs the second-best reliability of the 13, measured by repair bills, with the strongest resale value in its class, two of so many reasons it's one of the easiest-to-recommend used cars on the market.

What else to know. Refreshed in 2022, completely redesigned in 2026. The 2019–2025 generation isn't quite as refined as the other compact SUV benchmark, the Honda CR-V.

Honda CR-V

Honda CR-V

The CR-V is the RAV4's closest rival in every way that matters. It's roomy, efficient, well-built and satisfying to drive, with the best reliability of the 13 compact SUVs Bestest scores, measured by repair bills, and resale about as strong as anything on the road.

What else to know. Completely redesigned in 2023, refreshed in 2026. Used examples hold their value very well, so be prepared to pay more than you would for a comparable Tucson or Sportage.

Personality Plus

Mazda CX-5

Mazda CX-5

The CX-5 has long been the most stylish, most fun-to-drive compact SUV money can buy, and its interior punches well above its price.

What else to know. Refreshed in 2022, completely redesigned in 2026. The CX-5's back seat and cargo hold are tighter than most compact SUVs.

Mazda CX-50

The CX-5's newer, larger, more outdoorsy sibling, and the newest nameplate on this roster. It keeps Mazda's hallmark driving feel and upscale interior, but adds a more rugged stance and a bit of genuine trail capability.

Hyundai Tucson

Hyundai Tucson

The Tucson combines distinctive styling, a longer list of standard features than most rivals at the same price, and a longer-than-most warranty.

What else to know. Completely redesigned in 2022, refreshed in 2025. Stay away from the 2022 gas model, whose engine had a pattern of fuel-injector failures in the first year of the redesign.

Why only these seven?

We score every compact SUV on expert ratings, our own experience, reliability and resale value, incorporating trusted data from Kelley Blue Book, Edmunds, Car and Driver, Motor Trend, CarEdge and iSeeCars. Reliability is measured by repair bills: what insurers expect to pay to fix a used car bought with about 30,000 miles. Most used-car shoppers are (or should be) looking first and foremost for a reliable car that holds its value, so our proprietary algorithm weighs those measures higher than any others.
